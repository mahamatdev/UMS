🔴 End of Semester Examinations — June 2025
Urgent · Posted by Admin · 20 May 2025 · All Students
Final examinations for Semester 2, Academic Year 2024–2025 will commence on Monday, 28 June 2025. All students are required to be present with their student ID card. Timetables are available on the portal. Students with outstanding fees must settle their balances before the examination period.
🎓 Graduation Ceremony 2025 — Registration Open
Event · Posted by Registrar · 18 May 2025 · Final Year Students
The ISCAM Annual Graduation Ceremony 2025 is scheduled for Saturday, 12 July 2025 at the Main Auditorium. Final year students eligible for graduation must register via the portal before 30 June 2025. Gown fittings will take place at the Student Affairs Office from 1–5 July 2025.
📚 Library Extended Hours — Examination Period
Info · Posted by Library · 16 May 2025 · All Students
The university library will operate extended hours from 07:00 to 22:00, Monday to Saturday, during the examination period (15 June – 10 July 2025). Students may access digital resources 24/7 via the online portal using their student credentials.
💰 Fee Payment Reminder — May 30 Deadline
Notice · Posted by Finance Office · 14 May 2025 · All Students
This is a reminder that the deadline for payment of tuition fees for Semester 2, 2025 is 30 May 2025. Students who have not settled their fees will not be allowed to sit examinations. Contact the Finance Office for payment plans or assistance.
